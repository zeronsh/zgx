- **Atoms: chips that edit as one unit in a `ui.textarea`** (zgx, zgx-script): a range of the text (a `@file` or `/skill` mention) the caret never enters, one Backspace or Delete removes whole, a selection's end inside it moves out of, and that moves with the edits before it. Its label stays in the text, so copy, search, spellcheck and the accessibility value read plain text. Undo and redo bring a removed one back with its id and payload. `atoms = state` (two-way: `{ id, from, to, data?, style? }`, characters from 0) restores a draft's; `on_atoms = function(change)` hears what was added and removed (`removed` keeps the label and the range it had) and why (`edit`, `undo`, `redo`, `set`); `el:insert_atom(label, { id, data, style, replace })` makes one at the caret or over the `@que` typed for it; `el:atoms()` reads them; `atom_style = { bg }` washes them. In Rust: `TextEditor::{atoms, insert_atom, set_atoms, take_atom_changes}`, `EditorHandle::{insert_atom, set_atoms, on_atoms, set_atom_wash}`, `View::{atom_wash, on_atoms, atoms_when}`. First cut: the wash behind the label, drawn as the editor's other decorations are; padding, radius, an icon and a max width follow.
