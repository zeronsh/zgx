section: performance

- **A paragraph growing at its end costs the same each frame however long it is** (zgx, zgx-gpu): a streamed reply's paragraph, its text appended to (`RichText::appends`), was measured, laid out, drawn and damaged whole each frame, though only its end changed. Now its prepared text takes the last state's words, packed measurements and lines in place of copying them, and breaks lines again only from the last line the append can change; the renderer keeps that state's glyphs and quads to that line (`GpuStats::kept_text_lines`) and draws only the lines the frame's damage reaches; and the scene damages it from that line down (`Scene::set_rich_text_unchanged_above`, which `GpuRenderer::install_text` installs). A frame of 24 bytes appended to a 684 px wide paragraph: 284K instructions at 240 characters and 1,128K at 1,920 before (layout, damage and paint), 125K and 131K now. Text with underlines or backgrounds, padded runs, line clamps, more than one line of text (a line break), right-to-left or fallback-font text, and centred or right-aligned text that is not wrapped are drawn and damaged whole as before; the drawn frames are the same, to the pixel.
