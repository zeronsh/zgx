- **An aspect ratio sets an item's other side from the one flex, a size or a stretch gives it** (zgx): two `grow = 1, basis = 0, aspect = 1` images in a row are squares, as CSS's, where each kept its picture's own height (a 1200x1600 photo 200 wide and 1600 tall), and an empty box with one was as tall as nothing. An image with `aspect` alone is its picture's width and the ratio's height. One without `aspect` sizes as before.
