- **Sound in a 3D world** (zgx-3d, `audio` feature): `World::set_audio(&audio, &bus)` gives a world sound on a bus of the app's (the engine's `world` bus, which worlds share); `World::add_emitter3d` plays a zgx-audio sound from an object. It follows the object's place in the world, with distance, cone, equal-power panning, Doppler from frame-to-frame motion, and the air's dulling. The camera (or an object) listens. Meshes between them occlude it through each voice's own low-pass, cast as rays against the triangles picking uses, within a budget of rays and of triangles a frame, round-robin, only when an end or a mesh moved, down a tree of the meshes' boxes (8 rays over 4,000 meshes: 2 to 13 µs a frame); a mesh can let sound through (`World::set_sound_transmission`). Reverb zones fade in as the listener walks toward them. A still world sends no commands; a one-shot that ended is let go, and a world dropped stops its sounds.
