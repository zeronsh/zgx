- `ui.spring_to(state, target, opts)` runs a state to a value on a spring in Rust, with no script call per frame: for what a gesture hands over (its speed as `velocity`), with `from`, `delay`, `most` and `done`, and a `stop` that returns the speed it had. It rests within a thousandth of its range, so small ranges such as an opacity ease to their end.
