- **Full-width CJK text is as wide as Chrome lays it out on macOS** (zgx-gpu): an ideograph, CJK punctuation or kana advances as system-ui's PingFang UI faces do at that size (about 0.959 em at 14 px, not 1 em), so mixed Latin and Japanese lines wrap and measure as in a browser. Other platforms are unchanged.
