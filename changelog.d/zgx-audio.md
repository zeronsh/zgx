- **zgx-audio, a crate for sound** (zgx-audio): a mixer that places every play, stop and parameter change on an exact frame, whatever the device's block size. It has voices with gain, pan, pitch and a low-pass ramped a sample at a time, sends, buses in a tree with gain, mute, solo and fades, effects on buses (a compressor, a ducker, a reverb, an echo, filters and an EQ band, or an app's own through the `Effect` trait), and a look-ahead limiter on master. It reads WAV files, and streams or loads Ogg Vorbis, MP3, Opus and (on macOS) AAC behind features of their own. It has spatial maths (fall-off, cones, Doppler, panning), binaural output (`hrtf`), an offline renderer, and the system's default output (`device`). Whatever comes in, only finite samples come out. It has no default features, and zgx's crates reach it only through their own `audio` features, so apps that don't use sound pay nothing.
