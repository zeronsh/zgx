- **A line may break before an atom's icon** (zgx-gpu): an atom with a leading icon right after text, no space between ("…2010.pdf" then a chip), wraps to the next line whole, as the web's inline-flex chip does, rather than gluing to the text before it as one unbreakable word. Text next to text breaks where it did, and a mention still wraps at its own spaces with its icon on its first word.
