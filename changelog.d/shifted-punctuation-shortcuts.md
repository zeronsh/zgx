- **Shortcuts on punctuation and digits can be typed on any layout** (zgx): where a layout needs Shift to produce a shortcut's character (`/` is Shift+7 on German and Shift+: on French, whose digits need Shift too), the key arrived with Shift held and no binding matched it, so ⌘/ or Ctrl+1 couldn't be typed. A character with no letter in it that Shift only produced now matches as though Shift were up, as browsers and VS Code match it, once no binding takes the keystroke exactly: a binding that names Shift (`ctrl+shift+/`) still wins, and a letter's Shift is still its case.
