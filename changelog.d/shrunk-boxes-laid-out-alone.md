- **A box its column shrinks follows its content when laid out alone** (zgx): a box of a set height that a column too short for it held at its content's height kept that height after its content shrank, where its column shrinks it to the new one. And a box of a set size found, in the frame a box around it was laid out alone, to need a layout from the top was left as it was: what the outer box answered first, from the inner one's old answer, served that layout.
