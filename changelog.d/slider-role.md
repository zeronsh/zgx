- **Any element can be a slider to screen readers** (zgx, zgx-desktop, zgx-script): Luau's `role = "slider"` on a column, row, div or overlay, with `value`, `min`, `max`, `step` and `value_text` (all followed where they can change), `on_change(value)`, and `keyboard = true`. In Rust: `View::role(Role::Slider)`, `numeric_when` and `value_when`, plus the new `View::adjustable(step, on_change)` and `adjustable_keys`. VoiceOver, Narrator and Orca read the value, its range, its step and its value text ("High", not "3"), and increment, decrement and set it through `on_change`. With `keyboard`, the arrows, Page Up and Down, Home and End move it as `ui.slider`'s do, and it becomes focusable. `ui.slider` now tells screen readers its step and takes `value_text` too. On Linux, the value text is AT-SPI 2.46's `Value.Text` in builds from this repository, which patch in accesskit_unix. A build from crates.io, with upstream accesskit_unix, gives Orca the number, range and step without the text.
