- **Breaking: every script is equal** (zgx-script, zgx-run, zgx-dom): zgx has no idea of a plugin, a permission or a guard. A script loaded beside the root (`ScriptHost::set_scripts(vec![Script::file(name, path)])`, `scripts_in(dir, limits)`, zgx.json's `scripts`) reaches every host action, shared state, element, package and platform API the root does, within the `Limits` its host gives it (`memory`, a heap cap, `load`, `call`, `voices`, and `count_state` to count what its states hold against `memory`; none unless set; `Limits::app_budgets()` is what `zgx run` gives the root). Targets, filters, `ui.override`, slots and packages stay, as mechanism with no checks. An app that wants rules enforces them in its host: an action reads `ScriptHost::calling_script()`, a transport `Body::script()` and `Socket::script()`. Migrating: `set_plugins(vec![Plugin { name, entry, grants }])` becomes `set_scripts(vec![Script::file(name, entry)])`, `.plugins(dir)` becomes `.scripts_in(dir, Limits::default())`, `plugin_memory` becomes `Script::limits`; `Capability`, `grant`, `grant_all_plugins`, `expose_*`, `expose_hook`, `require_contribution_capability` and `reserve_prefix` are gone with nothing in their place; `guard`, `web_guard`, `keep_held_state`/`drop_held_state` and `FetchPolicy` are gone (a preview is plain hot reload; network rules go in the host's fetcher and connector); `ui.decision`, `ui.sealed`, `ui.plugin_page`, `decision_shown`, and an element's `private` and `hook = false` are gone (each an error saying so; `zgx lint`'s `removed-api` finds them; use your own components and slots); `Limits`' fields are `Option`s and `Limits::default()` has no budgets; zgx.json's `plugins` is now `scripts` and `expose` is gone; contribution reports and dropped shortcuts name their `script`; `forget_state` takes the script's name, and saved state moves from `plugin:<name>` to `script:<name>` once, by itself. A state provided into another script's node is now writable there.
