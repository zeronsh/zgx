- A selection that goes into part of a text area's atom (a chip) takes the whole atom, on the desktop and the DOM. A drag into a chip, a double click on one of its words and Shift+click all select it whole, so cut and delete take it whole as Backspace does. A caret alone still goes to the chip's nearer edge. This departs on purpose from the browser's `contenteditable=false` default, as chat composers' mentions do.
