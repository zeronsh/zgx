- **Text on macOS at 2x and over draws as Chrome's does** (zgx-gpu, zgx-cosmic-text): glyph masks come from Core Text as Skia draws them (black on white, font smoothing on, in a colour context at the point size under the device scale, so optical size and tracking follow the point size) and go through Chrome's coverage table for the text's luminance (four tables, chosen by the colour's top three bits a channel and Rec. 709 luma, measured from Chrome's own pixels: dark text thins a little, light text firms a lot). Text starts at its quarter pixel (subpixel positioning) instead of a whole one, and a line's baseline is worked out in device pixels (ascent and descent rounded, half-leading floored there), as Blink's layout does at 2x. Against headless Chrome 157's pixels, Geist at 14 px black on white is off by at most 3 levels a channel (swash's outlines were 13% lighter), as are white on dark and fractional x origins; SF at 14 px 400 to 700 is within 0.1% in ink except 510 to 590, which Chrome draws heavier (modelled by ink, not by pixels). Below 2x, other platforms and colour glyphs keep the earlier path. `tests/chrome_text.rs` holds four bands of Chrome's pixels.
