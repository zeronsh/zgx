- **Text on macOS at 2x and over draws as Chrome's does** (zgx-gpu, zgx-cosmic-text): glyph masks come from Core Text as Skia draws them (black on white, font smoothing on, in a colour context at the point size under the device scale, so optical size and tracking follow the point size) and are drawn without font smoothing, as Chrome draws text under `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased` (Anara's web build sets it: the plain coverage, no table; pixel for pixel at SF 14 px 400, 450, 600, 700). `zgx_gpu::set_font_smoothing(true)` draws as Chrome's default (`auto`) does: smoothing on, and four coverage tables by the text's luminance, measured from Chrome's pixels. Text starts at its quarter pixel (subpixel positioning) instead of a whole one, and a line's baseline is worked out in device pixels (ascent and descent rounded, half-leading floored there), as Blink's layout does at 2x. Against headless Chrome 157's pixels, Geist and SF match to a level or two a channel at fractional x origins and in light and dark. SF at weights 501 to 599, which Chrome emboldens, is not modelled. Below 2x, other platforms and colour glyphs keep the earlier path. `tests/chrome_text.rs` and `chrome_text_smoothed.rs` hold four bands of Chrome's pixels each.
