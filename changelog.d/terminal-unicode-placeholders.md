- **A terminal shows pictures placed by Unicode placeholders** (zgx-terminal): the kitty graphics protocol's virtual placements (`U=1`) and U+10EEEE placeholder cells, as tmux passes pictures on and programs inside it (yazi, image.nvim) send them. Each placeholder cell shows the cell of the picture its colours and diacritics name; they scroll and clear with the text, and draw no glyph.
