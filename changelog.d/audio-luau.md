- **Sound in Luau scripts** (zgx-script): the `audio` global loads sounds off the main thread (`audio.sound`), streams music (`audio.music`, `audio-streams`), plays voices on an exact frame (`at = audio.now() + 0.1`) and drives buses. `sound = click` on a button, checkbox or any element with `on_click` plays on the `ui` bus as it's activated, by pointer, keyboard or assistive technology; `click:with { gain, pitch }` sets its level and pitch, and `audio.choose(function)` picks the sound as the control is activated. A gain, pan or pitch can follow a state, a function or a `ui.tween`, with no script per frame. `voice.playing` turns false when the voice ends. A reload stops the old script's voices and streams. Effects on buses (`audio-effects`: `bus:add_effect(audio.low_pass { … })`, `reverb`, `delay`, `compressor`, `bus:duck`). Ogg Vorbis, MP3 and Opus come with `audio-vorbis`, `audio-mp3` and `audio-opus`. There are five lint rules (`audio-load-in-builder`, `audio-play-timer`, `audio-sound-too-long`, `audio-param-in-loop`, `audio-effect-in-builder`). The host gives scripts its engine (`ScriptHost::set_audio`). All of this is off by default: a build without these features is unchanged, its definitions and lint rules included. See docs/reference/audio.md.
- **Plugins play sound when granted `"audio"`** (zgx-script, `audio`): a plugin's `audio` plays into a bus of its own (`plugin:<name>`, under `ui`), its controls' `sound =` too, with at most 8 voices and 4 effects. Its sounds come from its own directory and count against its memory. It reaches no other bus, the master or the device.
