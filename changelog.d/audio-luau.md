- **Sound in Luau scripts** (zgx-script, zgx-desktop, zgx-run): the `audio` global loads sounds off the main thread (`audio.sound`), streams music (`audio.music`), plays voices on an exact frame (`at = audio.now() + 0.1`) and drives buses, and `sound = click` on a button, checkbox or any element with `on_click` plays on the `ui` bus as it's activated, by pointer, keyboard or assistive technology. A gain, pan or pitch can follow a state, a function or a `ui.tween`: one message a change, no script per frame, and a bus's gain sends none at all. `voice.playing` turns false when the voice ends, woken by the engine rather than polled. The `ui` bus follows macOS's "Play user interface sound effects". A reload stops the old script's voices and streams. Four lint rules (`audio-load-in-builder`, `audio-play-timer`, `audio-sound-too-long`, `audio-param-in-loop`). Behind `audio` features, off by default: `cargo run -p zgx-run --features audio -- app/`; an app without them is unchanged, and one with them opens no device until a sound plays. See docs/reference/audio.md.
