- **`ui.markdown_ended(key)` says a keyed Markdown text won't grow again** (zgx-script): what its next chunk would have needed goes at the next rest rather than at the first rest a minute later, which in a window left alone may be never. What is on show stays. An 800-section reply frees 3.7 MB of live memory, and 2.2 MB of footprint with `mimalloc`.
