- **Windows' caption buttons show everywhere, in the app's own ink, and agree when zoomed** (zgx-desktop, winit fork): they were an icon font, which a Windows 10 tester saw as empty boxes; they are now drawn as boxes and bars of whole pixels, with no font. They followed the system's light or dark, not the app's, so they were invisible on a light app on a dark system. They are now inked from the app's theme, as its own title bar controls are (`text.secondary` at rest, `text.primary` under the pointer, `text.tertiary` in a window in the back, over its `hover` and `pressed`; Close keeps its red), with an outline icon's line weight, so this changes how every Windows app's caption buttons look. winit's `set_theme` on Windows now updates `theme()`, and a system change no longer undoes it. In a zoomed window the buttons drawn, hit-tested and reported disagreed (at 150%: 207 pixels drawn, 138 clickable); all three, snap layouts included, now read one geometry and stay 46 by 32 points of the display, whatever the zoom. A window that isn't resizable, or whose smallest and largest sizes match, no longer shows Maximize or answers resize bands.
