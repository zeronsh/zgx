- **Rich text in the DOM** (`zgx-dom`): `rich_text` and `rich_text_signal` paragraphs show as text nodes, styled `<span>`s and `<a>` links, their looks shared classes. A web or mail link opens in a tab of its own unless its click handled it, hover styles are `:hover` rules, icons masked boxes, and `fade_in` a CSS animation. Spans that look as they did keep their nodes, so streamed text only appends.
