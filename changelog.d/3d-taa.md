- **A 3D world can smooth its edges with time: temporal antialiasing** (zgx-3d, `post` feature): `World::set_antialiasing(Antialiasing::Taa)` draws one sample a pixel, jittered over eight offsets, blended with the reprojected history (clamped in YCoCg to the frame's neighbourhood, Catmull-Rom history reads, depth-tested so what moved doesn't trail, blended in the tone curve's space). A still view settles finer than 4x multisampling in 8 frames and stops drawing; under camera motion still meshes leave no trail. `World::cut()` drops the history. Until the depth prepass, frames in which meshes moved lean on themselves (moving meshes aren't smoothed). A view shown in two boxes gathers its frames once a frame, each box size keeping a history of its own. Turning it off lets the world rest at once; a resize drag makes no history until the size stands, and a frame with a material animated by its time weighs as one in which a mesh moved. A view in more than four boxes of their own sizes smooths four and draws the rest as they are. Live particles weigh a frame as moving too, so small fast ones keep their light.
