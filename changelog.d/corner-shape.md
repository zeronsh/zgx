- **Rounded corners can take a superellipse shape the app sets** (zgx, zgx-gpu, zgx-dom): CSS's `corner-shape: superellipse(k)`, a continuous corner at 1.5. Round stays the default; a corner as large as half its box (a pill, a circle) stays round on the desktop. In the browser the box is given the same `corner-shape`.
