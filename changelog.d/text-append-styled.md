- **A streamed reply's styled paragraph, fading its words in, costs the same each frame however long it is** (zgx, zgx-gpu): the incremental path for a paragraph growing at its end now takes what a streamed reply's paragraph holds. Runs restyled or split and merged on its prefix (a fade's runs, `RichText::restyled_from`), inline code chips (padded runs) and words crossing runs (`**bold**text`) are laid out from their words; underlines and backgrounds are kept for the lines kept and made again from the first that changes; a frame that only recolours (a fade) makes its quads again from the first line it recolours, and is damaged from there down. A paragraph's links are placed from its laid-out lines (`Scene::rich_text_selections`), not shaped again each change. A frame of a reply as Zeron's transcript streams it (prose, prices, math and code in chips, bold, a link; 24 bytes a piece, each fading in as a run of its own): at 1,920 bytes, 3.95M instructions an append before and 0.24M now, 0.69M a fading frame before and 0.17M now.
