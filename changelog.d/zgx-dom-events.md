- **Listeners, keymaps and focus groups in the DOM** (`zgx-dom`): `on_event` (and `on_action`) listeners hear the browser's pointer, wheel, key, focus and activation events in their capture, target and bubble phases, their decisions applied to the event at once. Keymaps resolve keys with zgx's own resolution (contexts, sequences) before anything hears them and route their actions to listeners. Focus groups, list boxes, tab lists, toolbars and radio groups are one Tab stop moved by their arrows.
