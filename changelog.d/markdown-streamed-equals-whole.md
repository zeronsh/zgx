- **Markdown shows the same streamed as parsed at once** (zgx-script): a picture after a word in a list item stays among its words where a paragraph with a line break came before it; the indent of an HTML block no longer joins the next block's words, or makes a paragraph of its own in place of a list; and a link definition in a quote or list, or with its label across two lines, links what came before it as the reply streams. A property test streams random Markdown in random pieces, and compares with the text at once at every step.
