- **Animators play a model's clips in layers, by the world's frames** (zgx-3d, feature `gltf`): `World::add_animator(&placed)`, then `World::play(animator, &clip, Play::new().fade(0.25))` crossfades within a layer, and `World::set_layer` gives a layer a weight, a `Mask` of the nodes it moves (`Mask::below(node, weight)` for a subtree) and how it meets the layers under it (`Layer::new()` overrides them by its weight, `Layer::additive()` adds how far its clip moves from its first key). Clips loop or play once, at any speed, and report events (`Clip::with_event`, read with `World::animator_update`). Poses are a function of frame times, never the wall clock, so a video renders them the same each time. A playing animator keeps the world drawing; one paused, stopped or done lets it rest. Each frame samples a clip's key times once for all its channels and writes only the nodes that moved: posing a 19-joint character costs about a third of `Placed::pose` (560 instructions a joint a frame against 1,820). A world without an animator pays one check a frame. `Placed::node(index)` gives a node's object.
