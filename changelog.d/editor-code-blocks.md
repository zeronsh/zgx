- **A text area's code blocks scroll sideways and never wrap** (zgx, zgx-script): lines in one scroll group (`LineInset::scroll_group`, a textarea `line_insets` line's `group`) scroll sideways together under a sideways wheel or Shift and the wheel, shown inside the group's frame (`GroupFrame`, `EditorHandle::set_group_frame`); a `nowrap` line is shaped once at its full width, as the web's `white-space: pre` with `overflow-x: auto`. The offset moves a group's lines after their own layout, in a layer of their own the renderer copies: a sideways scroll shapes nothing. Carets, clicks and selection follow it, a caret moved past the frame brings it into view, `LineMark::pinned` keeps a block's wash and line numbers still, and `View::group_scrollbar` (`group_scrollbar = true`) shows the scroll views' scrollbar across the block that moved last. `EditorHandle::set_group_scroll`, `group_scroll`, `group_extent` and `on_group_scroll` read and move it (all hidden while they settle). A text area without groups lays out and draws as before.
