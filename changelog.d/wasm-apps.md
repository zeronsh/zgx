- **WebAssembly apps** (zgx-wasm, zgx-script, zgx-run): `ui.app(id, { wasm = "app.wasm" })` runs a component (`wasm32-wasip2`, the `zgx:app/app` world) inside the app's process on a thread of its own, sandboxed (no files, network, environment or time of day unless the host grants them; memory, each call's time and GPU use capped), and `ui.surface` shows it with all its input, as it does a program. It draws with WebGPU (render bundles, queries, stencil, overridable constants and async pipelines included) on a device of its own on the window's adapter, sharing frames with the window's device without a copy (Vulkan memory, IOSurfaces, Direct3D 12 handles; read back elsewhere), or presents CPU pixels; a trap, an exit, a call or GPU work running too long, or a lost device ends its session alone. Its shaders' loops are bounded per invocation and its submissions capped and run one at a time, so a GPU-hungry app can't hold the window's frames for more than a submission. Compiled modules are cached on disk (`set_wasm_cache`). A plugin needs `Capability::Wasm` (or `Apps`) and its module in its own folder; `ScriptHost::vet_wasm` and `GuardKind::Wasm` decide what runs. `crates/zgx-wasm/guest` has a Rust SDK and sample guests; `examples/wasm_apps` shows them. See docs/wasm-apps.md.
