- **Plugins add beside a list's rows** (zgx-script): `ui.contribute { target, before | after | replace }` and `ui.filter` at the root of a `ui.each` or `ui.list` row, which drew nothing, apply a row each: what is added lays out as the list's own items, keyed with its row (it follows a reorder and goes with a removal), and reads what the row's root provides, live.
