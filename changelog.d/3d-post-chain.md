- **A 3D world's frame can go through a post chain, starting with bloom** (zgx-3d, `post` feature): `World::set_post(Post { bloom: Some(Bloom::default()), .. })` draws the meshes in linear light into a float target and maps their tones in a last pass, with bloom mixed in: the scene blurred wider and wider (13-tap steps down with Karis's average on the first, tent steps up) and mixed rather than added, so it brings no light of its own. `Vignette`, `Grain` (a function of the frame's index, so recorded frames repeat) and `ChromaticAberration` are compiled into the last pass when on, as is `Grade`: white balance (temperature, tint), lift, gamma, gain, saturation and a 3D look-up table (`Lut::from_cube`, `Lut::from_strip`). `AutoExposure` adapts exposure to a histogram of the scene's light on the GPU, easing at separate rates up and down and settling within 1/64 stop. Apps add passes of their own with `World::add_post(PostPass::new(wgsl))`: a `post(p: PostIn)` function on every pixel, in the HDR stage or on what the display shows, with named parameters, checked at `add_post` and reported at the pass's own lines. A world with nothing on draws exactly as before.
