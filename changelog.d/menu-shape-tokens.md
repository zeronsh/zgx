- **Menus', popovers' and tooltips' shapes are theme tokens** (zgx): `menu.radius`, `menu.padding.x`/`.y`, `menu.border` and `menu.border.color`, `menu.item.padding.x`/`.y`, `menu.item.inset.x`/`.y` (how far an item's highlight sits in from the panel) and `menu.item.radius`; `popover.radius`, `popover.border` and `popover.border.color`; `tooltip.padding.x`/`.y`, `tooltip.border`, `tooltip.radius` and `tooltip.font.size`. Each defaults to the built-in look, which draws the same bytes as before; a theme, or a platform's look, sets them.
