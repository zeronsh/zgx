- **Sheen, anisotropy and iridescence** (zgx-3d): `World::set_sheen`, `World::set_anisotropy` and `World::set_iridescence` give a lit mesh velvet's grazing sheen, brushed metal's stretched highlights or a soap film's colours, as glTF's `KHR_materials_sheen`, `KHR_materials_anisotropy` and `KHR_materials_iridescence` say, and a model's materials bring theirs (their factors; not yet their textures). A world without them draws exactly as it did.
