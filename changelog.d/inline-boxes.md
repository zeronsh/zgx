- **Any view can sit in a line of text, as CSS's inline-block** (zgx, zgx-script, zgx-gpu): `rich_text().inline(view)` and Luau's `ui.rich { "Ask ", ui.row { … }, " now" }`, with `inline_box` for `vertical-align` and what it copies as. A box is measured by layout, never breaks across lines, grows its line as CSS's line box does, and keeps its own events, hover, focus and accessibility; a selection takes it as one character.
