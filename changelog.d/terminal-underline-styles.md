- **A terminal draws underline styles and colours** (zgx-terminal): double, curly, dotted and dashed underlines (SGR 4:2 to 4:5) and underlines in a colour of their own (SGR 58), as Neovim and other editors mark diagnostics, where every underline was one line in the text's colour. They are shapes the GPU draws under the cells, a curly one a wave a cell long; a plain underline is still the text's own, and a row without these draws as before.
