- **`zgx-fx`, an optional package of visual effects**: glow, pulse, ripple, wave, shimmer, sparkles, aurora, grain, electric, fire, motion blur; heat haze, shockwave, lens, clear glass, CRT, chromatic split, fluted glass over what's behind a node; liquid metal, mesh gradient, dither, grain gradient, metaballs, swirl, voronoi, waves, halftone and plastic fills.
