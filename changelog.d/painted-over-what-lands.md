- **A looping animation plays from its sheet under a panel that paints elsewhere** (zgx): what counted as painting over a box was any later box whose ink, the union of everything it holds, reached it, though nothing it held landed there: a window-wide panel with a shadow at one edge, or an overlay column, kept every declared loop under it drawing each tick (a chat's two "working" rings cost about 3% of a core this way). What it holds is now asked in turn, each inside its clip, and only what lands on the box counts; what is scaled, turned, blurred or shaded still counts its whole ink. Carets and native views over the window, which ask the same, follow.
