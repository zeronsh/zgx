- **Effects on buses** (zgx-audio): `Bus::add_effect` puts an `Effect` (the app's own, or a stock one as they land) at the end of a bus's chain, where it runs on the bus's sum before its gain. `Effect` is a public trait: `prepare` off the audio thread, `process` on interleaved frames in place, `tail` so the bus keeps running, and the device keeps playing, until a reverb or an echo has rung out. Parameters are set by index with `EffectHandle::set(index, value, ramp)`, kept to their range and smoothed by the engine, which gives the effect each frame's value. `Bus::add_effect_keyed` gives an effect another bus's level over the block before, for ducking. `zgx_audio::Checked`, installed as the global allocator, makes a debug build panic, naming the effect, when one allocates or frees on the audio thread. `Effect`, `Parameter` and `Context` keep the design's 0.x promise: not broken in a patch release, and in a minor release only with a changelog entry. An app with no effect pays nothing: the mixer's instructions with none are unchanged.
