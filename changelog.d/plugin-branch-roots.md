- **Plugins add beside a branch's root** (zgx-script): `before` and `after` at the root of a `ui.show`'s or `ui.switch`'s branch, or of a component, which matched nothing, are siblings in the parent's flow that come and go with the branch and read what the root provides, live. At a `ui.presence`'s content, the app's root or a window's root, which can't have siblings, they are an error in `ScriptHost::contributions` and to the plugin.
