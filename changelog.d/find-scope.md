- **`find_scope = true`**: find in page (⌘F, Ctrl+F) opened with focus in a node marked so, else last pressed in it, looks only within the nearest such node: its text, inputs' values and lists' rows, mounted or not, its bar at the node's top right (kept in the window however narrow the node). Opened again from another, it looks there; outside every one, in the whole window, as before.
