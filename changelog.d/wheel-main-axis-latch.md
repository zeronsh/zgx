- **A trackpad's sideways jitter on a vertical swipe stays with the list under it** (zgx): a scroll view moving along a wheel event's main axis takes the whole event and latches the gesture, as the latch reads the axis. The point or two sideways a vertical swipe carries went on to the views around a list and scrolled or stretched the page under it; across the main axis (a vertical swipe over a carousel) the other axis still goes on.
