- **`zgx inspect route x,y` says where a click there goes** (zgx-script): to a native view (a page, a terminal) or the interface, and what decided it: overlay paint or a listener in front, a disabled or hidden node, or a modal scope the page is outside of, named by its root. It also lists each page under the point with every node it is registered at, and whether that node shows. A page that ignores clicks can be diagnosed without clicking it.
