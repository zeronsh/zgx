- **macOS draws text as heavy as macOS does** (zgx-gpu, zgx-text, zgx-swash, zgx-cosmic-text): glyph outlines are thickened as CoreGraphics' font smoothing thickens macOS's own text, by the text's colour: dilated by `(0.0058 + 0.01 × level) × size` pixels (at most 0.3), the level the colour's linear-light luminance in quarters, values read from CoreGraphics. All text is heavier, light text most (off where the user turned smoothing off). Swash rasterizes it (zgx's swash fork, `Render::dilate_box`), so it costs what swash does; Core Text draws only the faces swash can't read. At 13 pt on Retina and from 32 px a glyph's ink is within 0.1% of Core Text's; swash's unhinted outlines drew text 13–26% lighter, thinner than every other app beside it. Colour glyphs (emoji) stay swash's; Linux and Windows are unchanged. `text::set_font_smoothing` lets an app follow the system's setting (the default) or draw text lighter, for a setting of its own: every window draws its text again.
