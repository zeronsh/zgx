# zgx-video media

"Every frame", the showcase of `zgx-video` (`crates/zgx-video/examples/showcase`), rendered headless with `zgx video render . every-frame.mp4 --motion-blur 4`: 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264 and AAC, 33 s. Its music is synthesized by the example's `make_score.py`.

- `every-frame.mp4`: the film.
- `shaders.gif`, `glass.gif`: two acts, for pull requests.
