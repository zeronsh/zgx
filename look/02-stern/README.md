# 02: the ship's stern

**Notice:** the two black triangles under the stern are gone; the stern is a flat transom panel above the water, curving into the hull's run, as a container ship's is (`50-crop-*` is the close view).

What was wrong: the hull's last section was closed by a fan of triangles from its centroid, which sat on the boot top's black band, so the fan shaded from black; and the section stayed deep and boxy to the end. Now the run rises over the last 18% to a transom foot a metre under the boot top, the sections turn to a rounded V over the skeg, and the transom is closed by strips facing aft in the topside colour (`examples/harbor/src/shapes.rs`, `hull`).

Still to come: the superstructure and funnel (plain boxes), a bulwark and railings, the hull's sheen (its clear coat reflects a flat sky; the engine's sky is coming).
